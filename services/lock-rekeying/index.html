Lock rekeying is one of the smartest security upgrades you can make after a move, lost key, roommate change, or employee turnover. Instead of discarding perfectly good lock hardware, a locksmith changes the internal pin configuration so your old keys no longer work and new keys do. A Good Locksmith has provided professional rekeying throughout Southwest Florida since 1994 for homeowners and businesses in Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Estero, Naples, and surrounding communities.

Rekeying restores control quickly and cost-effectively when lock bodies are still in good condition. It is a core part of our residential locksmith and commercial locksmith services, and it is often completed in a single visit.

If you are unsure whether rekeying or replacement is better, we inspect the hardware and explain the practical choice in clear terms before any work begins.

What Lock Rekeying Actually Does

Inside a typical pin tumbler lock are small pins that must align at a shear line for a key to turn. Rekeying replaces or rearranges those pins to match a new key bitting. The visible lock may look the same, but previously issued keys will no longer operate it.

That makes rekeying ideal when hardware is solid but access control has changed. New homeowners in Bonita Springs and Lehigh Acres rekey because they cannot know how many copies previous owners distributed. Landlords rekey between tenants. Offices rekey after staff changes.

When Rekeying Is the Right Choice

Rekeying is usually recommended when locks operate smoothly, show no major wear, and use standard keyways we can service. It is often the preferred option after:

  • Buying or renting a home or condo
  • Losing a purse, briefcase, or key ring
  • Ending a roommate, contractor, or caregiver arrangement
  • Employee or tenant turnover
  • Misplaced master or spare keys

If locks are failing, corroded, or using outdated low-security designs, replacement may be smarter. Our article on 5 signs it is time to replace your locks helps you spot those cases. We also offer lock repair and lock installation when rekeying alone is not enough.

Keyed Alike Convenience

Many Southwest Florida homeowners ask us to rekey several exterior doors to the same key. One key for the front door, side door, and garage entry door simplifies daily life and reduces the odds of locking yourself out with the wrong key set.

Businesses can combine rekeying with a structured master key system so managers keep broader access while staff keys remain limited. Property managers in North Fort Myers and San Carlos Park often use this approach across multiple units or amenities.

Rekeying After a Security Incident

If keys were stolen during a vehicle break-in or home burglary, assume those keys are compromised. Rekeying affected doors should be a priority, not an afterthought. Pair the locksmith visit with the practical steps in what to do after a break-in.

Seasonal residents preparing to leave town also rekey when they are unsure who may still have access from guests, cleaners, or previous ownership. Combine that appointment with tips from how to secure your home while traveling.

We evaluate each lock, confirm it is a good candidate for rekeying, and discuss whether you want unique keys per door or a keyed-alike package. Cylinders are re-pinned, locks are reassembled and tested, and you receive the new keys before we leave.
