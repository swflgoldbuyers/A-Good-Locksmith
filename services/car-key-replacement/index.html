Lost Key Versus Spare Key Appointments

A spare key appointment is usually planned: you still have a working key, and you want a duplicate before vacation, hurricane season, or handing a car to a teen driver. A lost key appointment is more urgent because you may be unable to start or even enter the vehicle.

For lost-key situations, we determine whether you need unlock service first, then key origination and programming. Drivers in Bonita Springs, Lehigh Acres, and North Fort Myers often call from home, work, or roadside locations where mobile service saves a tow.

While you wait, avoid ordering random key blanks online without programming support. An uncut or unprogrammed key will not solve an immobilizer-equipped vehicle.

Why a Working Spare Matters in Southwest Florida

Summer heat, tourist-season traffic, and busy family schedules make lockouts especially disruptive here. A verified spare key kept outside the vehicle is one of the simplest ways to avoid an emergency. Test that spare periodically; a forgotten spare that no longer works is not a spare.

Seasonal residents should make sure a trusted local contact has access instructions if a vehicle must be moved during a storm. Pair that planning with how to secure your home while traveling before you leave town.

After Theft or a Break-In

If keys were stolen from a home, gym bag, or vehicle, replace them promptly and consider whether house keys were on the same ring. Automotive key replacement may need to be paired with lock rekeying at home. Our guide to what to do after a break-in covers the wider checklist.
