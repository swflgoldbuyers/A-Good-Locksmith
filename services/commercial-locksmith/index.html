Master Keys and Controlled Access

Growing businesses quickly outgrow a handful of identical keys. A properly planned master key system lets managers open multiple doors while employees only open the spaces they need. That structure improves accountability and reduces the scramble that follows a lost key.

Our guide to commercial master key systems explained covers the basics. On site, we map doors, discuss who needs which access level, and implement a system that can expand as you add suites or storage areas. Combined with disciplined key issuance records, master keying is one of the most cost-effective commercial security upgrades available.

If a master key is lost, we can rekey affected cylinders and restore control without necessarily replacing every lock body on the property.

Minimize Downtime with Preventive Service

A failed lock at opening time costs more than a scheduled maintenance visit. We encourage commercial clients to address grinding cylinders, loose levers, misaligned latches, and worn keys before a complete failure. During an inspection we can also identify doors that still use outdated or easily compromised hardware.
